How to: Analyse your results

Once you have launched your Feedback Button, Targeted Survey, or Embedded Survey, you can dive into the data to gain actionable insights. This guide walks you through the Analyse Results dashboard, including how to view trends, filter data, and prepare exports.

Accessing Your Data

You can access the analytics for any active or past project directly from your main dashboard.

  1. Log in to your GetFeedback account.
  2. In the left-hand sidebar, select Feedback Button, Targeted Survey, or Embedded Survey.
  3. Find your project in the list and click the Analyse Results button under the "Action" column. Note this will only become available when a feedback form has been created!

The Analytics Dashboard

The Analytics tab provides a visual overview of your performance metrics. Key sections include:

Analytics Collective responses

Top — Frequency: a timeline chart showing the volume of responses received over the selected date range.

Analytics tab: charts related to each question and Metadata (Device, Operating System, Browser, Most active pages, IP Addresses).

Collective responses tab: raw, row-by-row list of individual responses (table view).

Section Description
Frequency A timeline chart showing the volume of responses received over your selected date range.
Mood Visualises the emotional tone of feedback using icons ranging from "Very likely" to "Very dislike."
Metadata Breakdowns of the technical environment of your respondents, including Device, Operating System, and Browser.
Identifiers Detailed tracking of Geographical data, source URLs, IP Addresses, and Emails.
Pro-tip: Use the Collective Responses tab at the top of the page to switch from visual charts to a line-by-line list of every raw response received.

Using Filters

To drill down into specific data sets, click the Filters button in the top right corner. The filter modal allows you to refine results based on:

  • Mood rating: View only positive or negative feedback.
  • Question type: Filter by specific survey interactions.
  • Demographics & Tech: Filter by Country, Device type, OS, or Browser.
  • Feedback URL: See responses coming from specific pages on your site.
Note: You can also toggle the "Show responses with Email" checkbox at the bottom of the filter menu to focus on identified users.

Exporting your data

If you need to perform external analysis or share results with your team, you can export your data directly from the dashboard.

  1. Apply any necessary filters to your view.
  2. Click the Export button in the top right corner of the page.
  3. The system will generate a file containing your response data.
Important: Any filters you have applied to your current dashboard view will also be applied to your export, ensuring you only download the data you currently need.
