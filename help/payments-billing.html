Your Billing Details, Invoices, and Payment History

Finding Your Billing Details

To get to your billing details:

  1. Log in to your GetFeedback account.
  2. Select Settings in the left navigation.
  3. Select Billing and transaction.
  4. Open the Billing Details section.

Your Billing and Transaction settings

The Billing and Transaction section of your Account Settings gives you an overview of the cost for your plan and when your next payment is due.

Item Description
Plan Type The set of features you have available in your account. Take a look at the Plans & Pricing details page for a guide to the features available (or not available) on each plan type.
Billing Frequency How often you need to make a payment, also known as your billing cycle. In other words, how often your plan auto-renews.
Next Billing Date When your paid plan either auto-renews or downgrades, depending on the auto-renew setting.
Auto-Renew If your subscription renews or downgrades at your next billing date. Learn more: Auto-Renew.
Next Billing Amount The amount due at the next billing date.

How You're Billed

If you sign up for an annual plan, you pay for a year up front. Your subscription will auto-renew every 12 months until you cancel it.

If you sign up for a monthly plan, you pay for the first billing cycle up front, then your subscription will auto-renew monthly until you cancel it.

If you're part of a team, see Team Billing Will there be a team billing page for self serve?for info on how consolidated billing works when paying for multiple accounts at once.

Invoices and Receipts

The Billing History section of your Account Settings has a record of your upcoming and past invoices. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact listed on the account.

If you pay for your plan by invoice, to keep your plan active, you need to pay any unpaid invoices before your next billing date. Instructions on how to pay and our bank information are located directly on the invoice itself.

To view an invoice or tax invoice:

  1. Log in to your GetFeedback account.
  2. Select the Profile dropdown in the upper-right corner.
  3. Select My Account.
  4. Go to the Transaction History tab.
  5. Select an invoice number to view an invoice, or select the document icon next to the invoice number to view the related tax invoice.

Payment Method and Billing Address

You can update your payment method and billing address in the Payment Information section of your Billing Details. The details that you can edit in your account depend on your payment method.

If your plan downgraded to a free plan, you need to re-upgrade your account to a paid plan to edit your billing details. You can update your payment info and billing address during checkout.

Payment Method Description
Pay Now If auto-renew is enabled and your payment method is stored here, it's automatically charged at your next billing date. You can update the payment method details at any time before your next billing date. The billing address should match the one associated with the payment method.
Pay by Invoice When you pay by invoice, we email you and your billing contact an invoice 45 days before your next billing date. You can update the billing address that will be listed on your invoice at any time before your next invoice is issued. We don't store the payment method you used to pay a previous invoice in your account, unless you paid by credit card and chose to store the card as your default payment method.

TIP! If you'd like to be emailed an invoice to pay rather than be automatically charged at your next billing date, you can pay by invoice.

Invoices

You can update the billing address that appears on your invoice in the Billing Information section of your Account Settings, as long as you edit this information before your next invoice is issued—changes you make to this section are only reflected on future invoices. Please contact us if you need to update an invoice that was already issued.

Billing Country or Currency

The currency you’re charged in depends on the billing country, plan type, and payment method. You can edit your billing country in the Billing Information section of your Account Settings. However, your billing country needs to match the country where your payment method is located.

In some situations, you might not be able to edit the billing country. Please contact us for more info.
