Team Management in GetFeedback

This article explains how to invite new users, manage users, and understand the differences between User Roles.

Admin Access Required: The Team Management panel is only accessible to users with the Administrator role. If you do not see the "Settings" button or the "Team Management" tab, please contact your account owner.

Accessing Team Management

To manage your organization's users:

  1. Click Settings in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar.
  2. Select the Team management tab at the top of the page.

User Management and Invitations

Your plan allows for an unlimited number of users. Users must be invited by an Administrator, and the current users on your team can be seen in the Your team members section.

Inviting a New User

  1. Go to the Invite team members section.
  2. Enter the Work Email address of the new user.
  3. Click Send invite.
  4. An invitation email will be sent immediately, and a confirmation toast will appear at the bottom of your dashboard.
Note on Email Validation: Invitations should be sent to valid company/work emails.
  • If an invitee already exists in the team or has a pending invite, the system will prevent a duplicate request and display an error message.

Managing Pending Invitations

If a user has not yet accepted their invite, they will appear in the list with an Invited badge.

  • Resend Invite: If the user cannot find their invitation email or the link has expired, click Resend invite. You will be asked to confirm before the new link is generated.
  • Cancel Invite: If you invited the wrong person or no longer need that seat filled, click Cancel. Once cancelled, the previous invitation link becomes invalid.

Understanding User Roles

When a user is part of your team, they are assigned one of two roles. Administrators can change these roles at any time using the dropdown menu in the team list.

Feature Member Administrator
Create & Edit Surveys Yes Yes
Analyze Feedback Data Yes Yes
Delete Surveys Yes Yes
Access Team Management No Yes
Manage Billing & Plans No Yes
Edit Account-Wide Settings No Yes

Member Role Restrictions

Users assigned the Member role have full access to all feedback collection tools (Feedback Button, Targeted Surveys, and Embedded Surveys). However, they cannot view the "Team Management" or "Billing" tabs within the Settings menu.

Managing the Team List

The Your team members table allows you to audit your account access:

  • Last Active: Tracks the last time a user logged into the platform. This is a helpful tool for identifying inactive users who may no longer need a seat.
  • Removing Members: To revoke access, click the Remove button.
    1. A confirmation modal will appear: "If you remove them, they'll lose access to the team and won't be able to collaborate or access data."
    2. Click Remove to confirm.
    3. A success toast will appear, and the seat will immediately become available for a new invite.

Troubleshooting for Invitees

If a user clicks an invitation link and sees a message stating "This invite link isn't valid," it is likely due to one of the following:

  1. The invitation has expired.
  2. The invitation was cancelled by an Admin.
  3. The link was already used to create an account.

In these cases, the user should contact their Administrator to request a new invitation.
