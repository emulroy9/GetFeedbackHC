Your password

This article explains how to change or reset your account password. Follow the steps below for a secure update.

Sign in

To begin, sign in to the account area by visiting https://www.surveymonkey.com/getfeedback/sign-inIs this the correct URL?

Change your password

Once signed in, open your account settings to update your password.

From the Settings page, go to Change Password. You can also use the following link to go directly to the Settings page to change your password: https://app.usabilla.com/member/account/settings#password

This option doesn't appear to exists in the Staging site yet

If you forgot your password

  1. On the sign-in page choose Forgot your password?
  2. Enter the email address for your account and follow the instructions in the password reset email.

Tip: Two-step authentication

We recommend enabling two-step authentication to protect your account.
