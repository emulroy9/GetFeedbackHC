Your password

This page explains how to sign in, change, or reset the password for your account. It also covers recommended password practices and two-step authentication.

Sign in

Open the account sign-in page at https://www.surveymonkey.com/getfeedback/sign-inIs this the correct URL?. If you are already signed in, skip to the Change your password section.

Change your password

To update your password when you know your current one:

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open your account Settings page.
  3. Choose Change Password and follow the prompts to enter your current password and a new password.

From the Settings page, go to Change Password. You can also use the following link to go directly to the Settings page to change your password: https://app.usabilla.com/member/account/settings#password

Forgotten password (reset)

If you can't remember your password, use the reset flow:

  1. Open the sign-in page and click Forgot your password?
  2. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the form.
  3. Check your inbox for a password-reset message and follow the link to choose a new password.

Password requirements and tips

  • Use a password of at least 8 characters (longer is better).
  • Include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Do not reuse passwords from other sites.
  • Store passwords in a reputable password manager.

Two-step authentication (2FA)

We strongly recommend enabling two-step authentication for extra account protection. When enabled you will be asked for a second verification step in addition to your password.

Single sign-on (SSO) or organisation accounts

If your organisation manages access centrally (SSO), you may not be able to change your password from this page — contact your administrator for help.

Troubleshooting

  • If you do not receive a reset email, check your spam/junk folder and ensure your account email is correct.
  • If the Change Password option is not visible, try in a private browser window or contact support.
  • For account lockouts or other access issues, contact Support (link at the top of the Help Centre).
Pro tip: Choose a long, unique passphrase and enable 2-step authentication when available.
