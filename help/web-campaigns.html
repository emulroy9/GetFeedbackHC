Creating and Launching Trigger-based Forms

Trigger-based forms are behavior-based forms that appear automatically when a visitor meets specific criteria. Unlike the standard "Always-on" Feedback Button, these forms are standalone and do not require a visible button to exist on the page.

1. Choosing Your Survey Type

To begin constructing a new campaign experience, click the global Create survey action control from your workspace header context menu. An overlay wizard modal titled "How should feedback appear?" will appear automatically on your screen template viewport grid.

Select the Trigger-based form selection option asset from the card grid, and click Continue. From there, select your required core layout format tile profile: choose either the subtle Slide-out survey card frame or the high-impact Full-screen design layout module before advancing straight into your editing canvas workspace environment.

  • Slide-out Survey: A subtle widget that slides in from the edge of the screen—good for quick check-ins without interrupting the user journey.
  • Full-screen Survey: A high-impact modal that overlays the page. Full-screen surveys can also be accessed via their own direct URL (visible in the Trigger-based Form overview), useful for linking in emails or support content.

2. Building the Form

Once you open the Build form view, you will be presented with a dynamic multi-page editor. Use the question sidebar menu on the left to add your chosen components directly onto your active page canvas.

The Multi-Page Tab Layout

The builder uses sequential tabs located at the top of the workspace to segment the user flow. The page types are outlined below:

  • The Start Page: The introductory experience for your users. Unlike other structures, Trigger-Based Forms do not force a specific mandated question type here—giving you total structural freedom right from the outset.
  • Intermediary Pages: Custom pages used to dive deeper into follow-up topics. You can add brand new pages into the flow by clicking the + (plus) icon next to the tabs.
  • The End Page: The final validation interface. When navigating to the End page, the question toolkit automatically limits available options strictly to Format elements (Header and Paragraph) and Advanced Settings, letting you construct a streamlined thank-you screen.

Adding Content and Question Types

Build your survey fields using the Add questions menu located on the left side of the dashboard. Click on any item to drop it directly into your active page layout:

  • Feedback: Select from NPS, Rating, or Mood (interactive emoji blocks).
  • Input: Capture user data using standard text input fields, descriptive Text area components, or designated Email blocks.
  • Choice: Provide guided inputs via structured Drop down selection elements, multiple-choice Checkbox controls, or single-select Radio buttons.
  • Format: Organize your content sections clearly using contextual Header text blocks and explanatory Paragraph blocks.
  • Advanced Settings: Manage back-end submission details directly below the question menu with options to toggle Store IP address and Store user location collecting features.

Page Logic and Control Visibility

Page logic is used to jump to specific pages depending on the answers given in the current page. Control visibility is used to shows questions on the same page based on the answer to a previous question. Page logic is accessed by clicking the 3 dots next to the page title and clicking edit, from here you can "Add Rule" in the page logic section. For control visibility you need to hover over a question and click the cog, you will see the control visibility options along the bottom. Note you cant used control visibility on the start or end pages

3. Targeting: Defining the "Who" and "Where"

Targeting rules allow you to control exactly when and to whom your form appears. From the Launch tab, navigate to the Targeting view within the "Show your form to certain visitors" option panel to specify deployment conditions. Targeting rules work as "AND" conditions—a visitor must meet all enabled criteria for the survey to trigger.

  • Visitors on a particular page: Restricts delivery strictly to a designated URL string or sub-directory match.
  • Visitor Status: Target specific segments by filtering for New or Returning visitors to your site.
  • Time on Page: Delays the form introduction until a user has been active on the current page layout for a specific amount of time (e.g., 5 seconds).
  • Scroll Depth: Triggers the interaction automatically when a visitor scrolls down to reach the bottom of the active page layout.
  • % of visitors: Reduces fatigue by limiting exposure to a specific random threshold of your overall traffic (e.g., 10% of visitors).
  • Exit Intent: Proactively captures feedback by deploying the form when the system detects a user is potentially exiting the page (e.g., within 5 seconds of exit trajectory). Note this is a Desktop only option and doesn't work on Mobile.
  • Page Views: Displays the form only after a visitor has navigated through a specific threshold of content pages (e.g., at least 3 pages).
  • Browser Cookies: Targets or excludes audiences depending on whether they carry a specific browser cookie payload.
  • Device Type: Limits form delivery to audiences using a specific hardware profile (such as mobile devices or desktop displays).

4. Scheduling & Fatigue Management

Within Launch the next option you will see is Schedule. This allows you to control the campaign lifecycle:

  • Activation: Choose to activate immediately, schedule a future start date, or save the campaign as "Off" to be toggled manually later.
  • Deactivation: Automatically stop the campaign on a specific date or after a certain number of responses (e.g., stop after 500 entries).
  • Reset Campaign: Define how often a user should see the survey again (e.g., every 30 days).
  • "Do Not Bother": By default our Trigger-Based Forms do not show again once they have been interacted with, however, this option uses Local Storage to ensure a visitor only sees the survey once until the next reset, whether they have interacted with it or not. This persists even if the user closes the tab, but will be cleared if they wipe their browser cache.

5. Installation

Once you have saved your form and options you will be brought to the Install page. Here you can find the installation instructions for you form:

  • The Snippet: Copy the unique JavaScript code and paste it into the global footer of your website. You will only need to install this on your site once, and it will allow the triggering of all of your Campaigns across your site.
  • CSP (Content Security Policy): If your site is highly secure, use the Include CSP Hash option in the Install tab to get the specific hashes required for your script-src and style-src headers. Learn more in our CSP Configuration guide.
  • Full-Page URL: If using the Full-screen format, you will also be provided with a direct Survey URL. This can be used as a standalone link in marketing emails or support signatures.

Pro-Tips for Success

  • Preview First: Use the Preview button in the builder to test your Page Logic and design before launching.
  • Avoid Overlap: Don't trigger multiple campaigns at the exact same moment on the same page; it can lead to a poor user experience and data conflicts.
  • Targeting Dependencies: If you only want to show Survey B to users who finished Survey A, use a custom cookie dropped on the "Success" event of Survey A.
