Compare GetFeedback plans, pricing, and savings so you can pick the right option for your team.

Overview

GetFeedback offers two subscription plans: Starter and Plus. Starter replaces the old Pro plan, while Plus replaces the old Premium plan.

Starter is intended for smaller feedback programs, while Plus is designed for growing teams that need advanced routing, collaboration, and analytics.

What’s included

Starter

  • 1,000 responses per month
  • Trigger-based forms for web campaigns
  • Multi-channel feedback collection
  • Core analytics for single-form reporting
  • Slack and email integrations, plus GA4 coming in Q3

Plus

  • 2,000 responses per month
  • Feedback Button and in-page embedded forms
  • Trigger-based forms and advanced campaign targeting
  • Multi-form analysis, sentiment analysis, and open-text extraction (planned in Q3)
  • App form support coming in Q3 for mobile passive and campaign-based experiences

Billing and renewal

Annual plans are billed once per year and give you the effective monthly savings shown above. Monthly plans are billed every 30 days and can be cancelled at any time.

If you choose annual billing, your subscription auto-renews at the end of each year unless you cancel before your renewal date.

How to choose a plan

  • Choose Starter if you need a simple feedback collection solution with essential reporting.
  • Choose Plus if your team needs workflows, team dashboards, advanced campaign targeting, or higher-touch support.

Need help selecting the right plan? Contact your account team or support to compare the plan details for your use case.

Tip: Annual billing is usually the best value if you plan to use GetFeedback long term, because it reduces the effective monthly price by about 17%.
