Compare GetFeedback Digital plans, pricing, and savings so you can pick the right option for your team.

Overview

GetFeedback Digital offers two subscription plans: Starter for smaller feedback programs, and Plus for teams that need advanced routing, collaboration, and reporting features.

What’s included

Starter

  • Feedback Button and embedded form collection
  • Basic feedback workflows and form customization
  • Core analytics and reporting dashboards
  • Standard support and account access controls

Plus

  • Everything in Starter, plus advanced routing and review workflows
  • Trigger-based forms and web campaign targeting
  • Team management and shared dashboards
  • Priority support and billing controls

Billing and renewal

Annual plans are billed once per year and give you the effective monthly savings shown above. Monthly plans are billed every 30 days and can be cancelled at any time.

If you choose annual billing, your subscription auto-renews at the end of each year unless you cancel before your renewal date.

How to choose a plan

  • Choose Starter if you need a simple feedback collection solution with essential reporting.
  • Choose Plus if your team needs workflows, team dashboards, advanced campaign targeting, or higher-touch support.

Need help selecting the right plan? Contact your account team or support to compare the plan details for your use case.

Tip: Annual billing is usually the best value if you plan to use GetFeedback long term, because it reduces the effective monthly price by about 17%.
