Configuring Content Security Policy (CSP) for GetFeedback

As security and privacy become top priorities, many organizations use a Content Security Policy (CSP) to reduce the risk of XSS (Cross-Site Scripting) attacks. A CSP header tells the browser which dynamic resources (such as JavaScript, Stylesheets, and Images) are allowed to load. If your website uses a strict CSP, you may need to add GetFeedback to your "allowlist" to ensure your feedback buttons and surveys load and function correctly.

Checking for CSP Issues

If you have installed the GetFeedback snippet but your feedback button isn't appearing, or the form looks unstyled, you may have a CSP conflict.

To verify this:

  1. Open your website in Google Chrome.
  2. Right-click anywhere on the page and select Inspect, then click the Console tab.
  3. Look for red error messages. A CSP error will typically state that a resource failed to load because it "violates the following Content Security Policy directive."

Required CSP Directives

To ensure full functionality of our web-passive solution, your technical team should update your CSP to include the following directives. These allow the browser to fetch the necessary scripts, styles, and frames from our secure domains.

  • script-src: Allows the JavaScript required to trigger and run the feedback forms.
  • img-src: Allows the images and icons used within your custom themes.
  • style-src: Allows the CSS required to style your buttons and forms. Note that GetFeedback requires 'unsafe-inline' for some styling elements.
  • frame-src: Allows the iFrames where the feedback solution "lives" on your page.
  • connect-src: Allows the browser to send the collected feedback data back to our servers.
  • font-src: Allows for custom fonts or Google Fonts used within your themes.

Basic CSP Template

Below is a standard configuration that includes the GetFeedback (Usabilla) domains. You can provide this snippet to your web developers as a starting point:

script-src 'self' 'sha256-<YOUR_HASH>'
  https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net *.usabilla.com;
img-src 'self' https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net
  https://*.usabilla.com;
style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'
  https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net *.usabilla.com
  https://fonts.googleapis.com;
frame-src 'self' https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net
  *.usabilla.com;
connect-src 'self' https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net
  *.usabilla.com;
font-src 'self' https://d6tizftlrpuof.cloudfront.net
  *.usabilla.com https://fonts.googleapis.com;

Troubleshooting: Style Restrictions

In some highly secure environments, organizations do not allow a "static nonce" or 'unsafe-inline' within the style-src directive.

If your security policy restricts these, please be aware of the following impact:

  • What won't work: The feedback button and the feedback form will lose their custom styling (they may appear as plain text or broken elements).
  • What will work: Active Trigger-based forms and code-related features will continue to function.

Workaround

If you cannot modify your style-src directive to accommodate GetFeedback, a custom technical implementation may be required. This typically involves using additional JavaScript or CSS to manually manage the usabilla_live_button_container after the solution has initialized on your page.
