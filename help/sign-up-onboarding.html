Sign Up, Log In, and Inviting Your Team

Setting up your GetFeedback account is a quick and simple process. Whether you are creating a new account from scratch or joining an existing team, this guide will help you get through the front door.

Signing Up and Logging In

To create a new account, simply head to the GetFeedback Homepage and click the Sign up button in the top-right corner.

Authentication Options

You can choose to create an account using your professional work email or by using Sign up with Google for a faster, one-click experience.

Choosing Your Plan

After you have completed the initial sign-up steps and verified your email, you will be redirected to our Plans page. Here, you can select the subscription level (such as Web Pro or Web Premium) that best fits your digital experience goals.

Classic Login

If you already have an account, use the Log in button on the homepage to enter your credentials or sign in via Google.

Inviting New Users

If you are an Administrator and want to bring your colleagues into the fold, you can do so directly from your dashboard.

Where to Invite

Navigate to Settings in the bottom-left corner of your site, then select the Team management tab.

How it Works

Simply enter your teammate's work email address and send the invitation. They will receive an email to join your organization and set up their profile.

Need more help with roles and permissions? For a deep dive into managing your colleagues, check out our dedicated Team Management article for more detail on user roles, seat limits, and invitation status.
